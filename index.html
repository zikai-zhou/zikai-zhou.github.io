Accountable AI

An AI system is accountable when its decisions can be examined, justified, and contested: we can see what decided it, check that its explanation is actually true, and know what would change the outcome. I pursue this along three routes: by construction, where language models translate rules into formal constraints and a solver decides (VERDICT, SatIR); by training, teaching models to report what actually changed their answers (CREDIT); and by measurement, scoring what models say about themselves against what happens when their inputs change.
