About

I am a Computer Science PhD student (2024–) at Stanford University, advised by the amazing Prof. Monica S. Lam. I am affiliated with the Stanford Open Virtual Assistant Lab and the Stanford NLP Group. I build accountable AI systems whose decisions can be verified, challenged, and changed. Previously, I received my undergraduate degree from the University of Chicago (2020–2024).

I'm actively looking for collaborators and research assistants to work on accountable/faithful AI and its applications to healthcare. Feel free to reach out if you're interested!
